# Design Brief: Rupam Precision

## Visual Direction
- **Extravagant, cinematic, bespoke industrial website** with strong art direction.
- **NO CARDS:** No rounded feature cards, 3-column service-card grids, boxed industry tiles, floating dashboard panels, glassmorphism cards, etc.
- **Typography:** Oversized editorial typography.
- **Imagery:** Dramatic, edge-to-edge imagery.
- **Composition:** Asymmetrical compositions, large visual transitions, full-width capability rows/chapters.
- **Color Palette:** Dark industrial foundation with lighter technical sections for contrast.

## Tone & Story
- Precision-engineering brand experience.
- Restrained measurement graphics and technical annotations.
- Ethereal Machines is a directional reference (industrial clarity), not a source to copy.

## Motion Guidelines
- GSAP with ScrollTrigger as the primary animation system.
- Three signature moments: Hero introduction, Capabilities sequence, Drawing-to-delivery story.
- Native scrolling initially (no scroll hijacking).
- Reduced-motion support and normal readable mobile layouts.
